HOW TO PLAY

PC KEYBOARD CONTROLS

W / Up Arrow: Accelerate / Drive Forward

S / Down Arrow: Brake / Reverse

A / D / Left & Right Arrows: Steer

Spacebar: Handbrake (Hold to initiate drift!)

Left / Right Shift: Nitro Boost (N2O)

C: Change Camera View (Close, Far, Hood, Cockpit)

R: Reset / Respawn Car

M: Toggle Audio Mute

Escape: Pause Game

MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS

Left / Right buttons: Steer left and right

GAS pedal: Accelerate

BRAKE pedal: Brake & Reverse

DRIFT button: Break traction for smooth drifting

NITRO button: Rocket boost speed

PRO RACING TIPS

• Tap Handbrake when entering sharp corners to initiate a drift.

• Drifting charges your Nitro tank faster!

• Fire Nitro on long straightaways to blast past opponents.
